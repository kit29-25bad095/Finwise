## 3. NON-NEGOTIABLE FINANCIAL AUTHORITY ISOLATION

Strict boundaries prohibit AI from performing financial mutations:
1. **Zero Financial Computation by AI:**
   - LLMs are strictly forbidden from calculating expected revenue, settlement amounts, fees, GST, refund discrepancies, or recovery figures.
   - All financial amounts are computed deterministically by Phase 3 (reconciliation) and Phase 4 (leakage detectors).
   - Floats and IEEE 754 numbers are strictly rejected; arbitrary-precision `Decimal(18, 4)` is enforced throughout.
2. **Zero Automated Mutation or Resolution:**
   - AI cannot resolve, transition, or close leakage exceptions.
   - AI cannot approve or disburse refunds or recoveries.
   - Claim drafts generated by Phase 5 are saved with `status = "DRAFT"` and require explicit human-in-the-loop review and submission.
